# IronBeat – User Guide

IronBeat is your private workout soundtrack: type what you want, tap, and the music starts.

## 1. Sign up (30 seconds)

1. Open the IronBeat link in your browser.
2. Tap **Create an account**, then enter your name, email and a password (8+ characters).
3. Tap **Create account & start**. There is no approval step; you are in immediately.

Your playlists, liked tracks and search history belong to you alone. No other user can see them.

## 2. Add IronBeat to your iPhone Home Screen

Use **Safari** (other iOS browsers cannot install web apps).

1. Open IronBeat in Safari and sign in.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow) at the bottom of the screen.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Keep the name **IronBeat** (or rename it) and tap **Add**.

The IronBeat icon, a dark tile with an amber dumbbell, appears on your Home Screen. It fills the whole tile, so nothing is cropped and there is no stray border. Launch it from there and the app opens full screen like a native app, with no Safari bars.

If the icon ever changes and your phone still shows the old one, remove it from the Home Screen and add it again.

## 3. Search instantly

- The search bar is the first thing on the Home screen.
- Type anything, such as `workout nasheed`, `brazilian phonk` or `gym motivational speech`, and tap **Pump** (or press Search on the keyboard).
- The first result starts playing right away and the rest are queued. When one track ends, the next starts automatically.
- Tap any other result to jump to it; the tracks after it become your queue.
- Beside each track: ♥ **like** it, or tap the list icon to add it to a training folder.

## 4. Quick-launch chips

Every search that finds music becomes a chip under the search bar. Swipe the row sideways and tap a chip to relaunch that search and start the music instantly. Use **Clear recent searches** to reset the chips.

## 5. Moods (Vibe deck)

The **Vibe deck** sits on the Home screen below the search bar; scroll down to see every card. Each card is a ready-made training energy:

⚡ Phonk & Hype · 🎧 Focus & Nasheed · 🔥 Aggressive Lift · 🧊 Cool Down · 🔊 Heavy Bass · 🦍 Beast Mode · 🏃 Cardio Flow · 🌆 Synthwave Energy · 🎤 Gym Speeches · 🎤 Hip-Hop Grind

Tap a card and a freshly mixed playlist starts playing. It is a different mix every time.

## 6. Training folders (playlists)

1. Open **Folders**, type a name such as "Leg Day" and tap **Create**.
2. On any track, tap the list icon and pick a folder (or create a new one on the spot).
3. Open a folder to play it, shuffle it, rename it (pencil), delete it (trash) or remove single tracks (×).

### Smart Shuffle

Tap **Smart Shuffle** on Home or Folders. IronBeat builds a mix from your liked tracks and the genres you search most. The more you search and like, the better it gets.

## 7. The player

- A mini-player sits above the bottom bar on every page, with title, thumbnail, play/pause and skip. Tap it to open the full player with seek bar, shuffle, repeat, like, add-to-folder and the "Up next" list.
- **Lock screen and AirPods:** play, pause, next and previous work from the iPhone lock screen, Control Center and AirPods. Tracks from the open sources (Internet Archive, Openverse, Jamendo) play in the background with full controls. YouTube tracks cannot play with the screen locked on iPhone (Apple stops embedded video). **Settings → Background-safe mode** (on by default on iPhone) skips YouTube tracks so music keeps playing when you lock the phone or leave the app.

## 8. Switch themes

Tap the round colour swatch in the top-right corner of any page (or open **Settings**) and choose:

- **Warm Neutral**: soft cream, charcoal cards, amber/gold accents (default)
- **Monochrome Ink**: high-contrast black and white
- **Sunset Glow**: cream with sunset-orange accents

Your choice is saved to your account and follows you across devices.

## 9. Sign out

Open **Settings** and tap **Sign out**.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Tap play to start the music"**: your browser blocked autoplay. Tap the play button once.
- **A track skipped by itself**: its source blocked playback, so IronBeat moved on to the next one.
- **Few or no results**: try different words, or ask whoever runs the app to enable YouTube search.
