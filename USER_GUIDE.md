# IronBeat — User Guide

IronBeat is your private workout music vault: search, tap, lift. This quick walkthrough gets you from sign-up to your first set.

---

## 1. Sign up (30 seconds)

1. Open the IronBeat link in **Safari** on your iPhone.
2. Tap **Create an account**.
3. Enter your **name**, **email** and a **password** (at least 8 characters).
4. Tap **Create account**. You are in immediately — no approval needed.

Your playlists, liked tracks and search history are completely private to your account. Already registered? Use **Sign in** with the same email and password.

---

## 2. Add IronBeat to your iPhone Home Screen

Doing this makes IronBeat open full screen like a native app, with its own icon.

1. Open IronBeat in **Safari** (it must be Safari, not an in-app browser).
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up) in the bottom toolbar.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. You'll see the **IronBeat icon** (a gold dumbbell on charcoal) and the name “IronBeat”. Edit the name if you like.
5. Tap **Add**.

The icon now sits on your Home Screen. Tap it to launch IronBeat full screen, with no browser bars.

**Tips**
- The icon is designed to fill the whole iOS icon tile, so it looks crisp with no borders.
- If you ever see an old icon after an update, delete it from your Home Screen and add it again.
- Open the app once while online so it can set itself up.

---

## 3. Switch themes

Pick the look that fits your gym lighting.

- **Quick switch:** on **Home**, tap the **palette button** (top right) and choose a theme from the dropdown.
- **Settings:** open the **Settings** tab and tap a theme card.

| Theme | Look |
|---|---|
| **Warm Neutral** (default) | Soft cream background, charcoal cards, warm amber/gold accents |
| **Monochrome Ink** | High-contrast black and off-white, editorial and minimal |
| **Sunset Glow** | Cream background with bold sunset-orange accents |

Your choice is saved to your account and applies on every device you sign in on.

---

## 4. Search instantly

The search bar is at the top of **Home**.

1. Type anything: *“workout nasheed”*, *“brazilian phonk”*, *“gym motivational speech”*.
2. Tap **Play** (or press Search on the keyboard).

The first result **starts playing right away**, and every other result is **queued automatically**. When a track ends, the next one starts on its own, so you never touch your phone between sets.

- Tap any result to jump to it (the rest of the list follows).
- Tap the **heart** to like a track.
- Tap **⋯** for more: add to a playlist, play next, or add to the queue.

---

## 5. One-tap recent search chips

Under the search bar is a scrolling row of your **past successful searches**. Tap a chip to start that search and music instantly — no typing. Swipe sideways to see more. The most recent searches come first. (New accounts see a few suggestions to get started.)

To wipe your history, go to **Settings → Clear search history**.

---

## 6. Mood playlists (the Vibe deck)

On **Home** you'll see mood cards, and the **Moods** tab shows them all:

⚡ Phonk & Hype · 🎧 Focus & Nasheed · 🔥 Aggressive Lift · 🧊 Cool Down · 🔊 Heavy Bass · 🦁 Beast Mode · 🏃 Cardio Flow · 🌆 Synthwave Energy

Tap a card and a curated mix starts instantly. Each tap picks a fresh search for that vibe, so the playlist is different every time.

---

## 7. Controls: mini-player, full player and lock screen

- **Mini-player:** a bar above the bottom tabs shows the current track with **play/pause** and **skip**. It stays visible on every page.
- **Full player:** tap the mini-player to open the big artwork, **seek bar**, **previous / next**, **repeat**, a **like** button and the **Up next** queue (tap to jump, ✕ to remove).
- **Lock screen & AirPods:** while music plays you can play, pause, skip and scrub from the iPhone lock screen, Control Center and AirPods.

> Tracks from YouTube (when enabled) can pause when the screen locks — that's an iOS restriction. Other sources keep playing.

---

## 8. Build your training folders (playlists)

1. Open the **Playlists** tab.
2. Type a name (for example *Leg Day*) and tap **Create**.
3. Search for music, tap **⋯** on a track → **Add to playlist** → pick your folder. You can also create a new folder right from that menu.
4. Open a folder to **Play** or **Shuffle** it. Use the **pencil** to rename it, the **trash** to delete it, and **⋯ → Remove from this playlist** to remove a track.

Your **Liked tracks** (everything you've hearted) live at the top of the Playlists tab.

### Smart Shuffle

Tap **Smart Shuffle** (on Home or Playlists) for a dynamic mix built from **your most-searched genres and your liked tracks**. The more you search and like, the better it gets. Each tap builds a fresh mix.

---

## 9. Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Nothing plays on the first tap | Tap play again; iOS sometimes needs one extra tap after opening the app. |
| A track is skipped automatically | It couldn't be played from its source, so IronBeat moved on to keep your workout going. |
| “No tracks found” | Try a broader search (a genre or artist name). |
| Music stops when the screen locks | Likely a YouTube track — choose a result from another source, or keep the screen on. |
| Signed out unexpectedly | Sessions last 30 days; just sign in again. |

Sign out any time from **Settings → Sign out**.
